1. MODELI DANNYKH (backend/app/models/)  
--------------------------------------------  
1.1 ORDER (order.py) - Bazovaya tablica prikazov  
Polya:  
- id: Guid (PK)  
- number: string  
- date: DateOnly  
- type: OrderType (enrollment, expulsion, next_course, academic_leave, transfer_direction)  
- reason: string  
  
1.2 ORDER_STUDENT - Svyaz studentov s prikazami  
Polya:  
- id: Guid  
- order_id: Guid (FK)  
- student_id: Guid (FK) 
  
1.3-1.7 SPETSIALNYE PRIKAZY:  
- EnrollmentOrder (zachislenie)  
- ExpulsionOrder (otchislenie)  
- NextCourseOrder (perevod na sled. kurs)  
- AcademicLeaveOrder (akadem)  
- TransferDirectionOrder (perevod na dr. napravlenie)
